Welcome to AuraBudget

100% Free Forever • Zero Tracking

What It Is

A fast recurring cashflow hub designed to track subscriptions, groceries, medicines, car upkeep, deliveries, and fixed expenses with urgency prioritization.

Who It Is For

Anyone managing regular disbursements who wants an interactive 40-day projection to anticipate upcoming expenses before renewals happen.

Local Snapshot Notice

Your data is stored strictly in your browser (IndexedDB). Always use the Backup & Restore button to download JSON/CSV backups.
